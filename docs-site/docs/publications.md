## Journal Articles

- S. A. Zamperini, T. N. Bernard, S. Andersen, A. C. D. Hoffmann,
  D. L. Rudakov, and T. Abrams, "Fully generalized, turbulent trace impurity
  transport with Gkeyll and Flan in the DIII-D far-SOL," *Plasma Physics and
  Controlled Fusion* **68**, 075013 (2026).
  [doi:10.1088/1361-6587/ae836b](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6587/ae836b)

  The first official Flan publication. Validation tests are shown, and tungsten
  transport in the DIII-D far-SOL is simulated. Main takeaways are an early
  attempt at extracting the anomalous diffusion and pinch velocities as well
  as the observation that transport away from the wall increases with atomic
  number.

- S. A. Zamperini, T. N. Bernard, D. L. Rudakov, and J. A. Boedo, "Turbulent
  drifts of impurity ions as an explanation for anomalous radial transport in
  the far-SOL of DIII-D," *Nuclear Fusion* **64**, 074002 (2024).
  [doi:10.1088/1741-4326/ad4c78](https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-4326/ad4c78)

  A python-based precursor to Flan that tracked impurities via their guiding 
  centers. It became clear that the guiding center approximation was violated
  because the fluctuations were on the same scale as the tungsten gyroradius,
  and that the full gyro-orbit needed to be resolved. This was the key issue
  that showed the need for a proper high-performance code that resolved the
  gyro-orbit and all the additional Monte Carlo structure.
